  Parameters are already enumerated exhaustively.
- Each hypothesis must name WHO IS STRUCTURALLY PAYING and why they keep doing
  it. "It backtests well" is not a mechanism and will be rejected.

  of the best cell, so an axis with only two levels has no "one step away".

  and neither do strings -- a gate axis of "sma:20" through "sma:200" reads as

  H0007 cost 126 trials between them before this was enforced. If the axis you
  care about is naturally a string, express it as a number in `fixed` plus a
  numeric grid axis, or propose the numeric form of the same question.
- Keep the grid small enough to be justified. Every configuration you enumerate
  enters an append-only ledger and raises the multiple-testing bar for every
  result the project will ever produce. An axis that cannot plausibly change
  the outcome is a cost with no return.
- Set "track" to "A" for accumulation shapes or "B" for signal-driven positions
  entered long or short and exited on their own signal.
- Never propose opening the sealed holdout.

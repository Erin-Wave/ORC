Write the entry in Markdown. Be specific and quantitative. Use these sections.

## What was claimed
One paragraph: the mechanism, and who was said to be paying.

## What happened
The numbers, with the disqualifiers attached. Never quote a headline figure
without its shape, its independent-path count and its PBO beside it.

## Why it broke
This is the part that matters. Not "the backtest was bad" -- the specific
mechanism by which the claimed edge failed to survive. If the gate results say
something the headline number did not, say what, and say what the gate saw that
the headline hid.

## What this closes
Which questions are now answered, and which neighbouring ideas are also dead
because they share the failing mechanism. Be strict: a family closing does not
close everything near it, but it does close everything that fails for the same
reason.

## What remains open
What the failure does NOT rule out. If a variation could plausibly survive,
name the variation and the specific reason it might.

Do not propose a new hypothesis. That is the reasoning layer's job and it
happens against a cycle report, not against a post-mortem.
